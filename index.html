About hustle

Hustle is a partner social dance created in the 1970s by Latine, Black, and LGBTQ+ communities of the Bronx, NYC. Sharing roots with Mambo and Salsa, it was first danced in the clubs to disco, funk, and later, house. In Hustle, leading and following aren’t tied to gender. Many in the community dance both, and you’ll often see partners switch roles halfway through a song.

Never danced before? Our classes are beginner-friendly, and the goal is simple: to get you out on the club floor, dancing like it’s 1975.
