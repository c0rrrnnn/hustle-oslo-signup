6. Right of withdrawal (angrerett)

There is no right of withdrawal for these bookings. A Hustle Oslo class is booked for a specific date and time. You can still rebook under our own policy in section 7.

7. Cancellation, rebooking and refunds

7.1 If you cancel

  • We do not give refunds when you cancel, but you can rebook free of charge (once) to another Monday if you cancel at least 24 hours before class start (e.g. by 18:00 on Sunday for an 18:00 class).
  • The rebooking must be used within 2 months of the original class date. After that, the booking expires.
  • If you cancel less than 24 hours before class start, you cannot rebook, but you may transfer your ticket to a friend for the same night (see section 3).
  • How to cancel or rebook: email hustleinoslo@gmail.com with your name, the date you booked (shown in your confirmation email) and, if you know it, the new date. The time we receive your email counts. We confirm by email.

7.2 If Hustle Oslo cancels or changes a class

  • If we cancel a night (for example because of teacher illness or venue problems), you get a full refund, or a free rebooking if you prefer. We tell you by email and on Instagram @hustleoslo as early as possible.
  • If we have to change the teacher, or make a small timing change (up to 15 minutes), the booking stands. If the change is significant and you can no longer attend, you may ask for a full refund.
  • We are not liable for other costs (such as travel) caused by a cancellation, except where Norwegian law says otherwise.

7.3 How refunds are paid

Where a refund applies, it is paid back to the same payment method you used (Vipps), without fees, within 7 working days. Your bank may need a few extra days to show it.

7.4 No-shows

If you don't come and haven't cancelled in time, there is no refund or rebooking.

8. Returns

A class is a service, so there is nothing to return. Cancellations, rebooking and refunds follow section 7.
